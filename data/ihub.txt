SCSU Innovation Hub

The Innovation Hub at SCSU connects students with entrepreneurship, research, and tech innovation opportunities. It provides co-working space, startup mentorship, workshops, and funding support for student-led projects. The hub collaborates with faculty, industry professionals, and community partners to support innovation and career pathways.

Key Programs and services:
- Career coaching
- building resume and cover latter
- Innovation Workshops
- Research Collaboration
- Student Co-working and Lab Space

Deadlines:
- Innovation competitions held annually
- Program applications vary by semester
