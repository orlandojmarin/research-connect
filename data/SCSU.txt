
Southern Connecticut State University

(SCSU) is a public university in New Haven, Connecticut focused on academic excellence, career development, research, and community engagement. The university offers over 225 undergraduate and graduate programs and highlights affordability and access to education. SCSU supports hands-on learning through internships, labs, research centers, and industry partnerships. The homepage showcases campus news, event announcements, and admissions information.

Key Programs:
- Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Business, Education, Health, STEM, and Arts & Sciences
- Research programs and grant-funded projects
- Honors College and Study Abroad options

Deadlines:
- Undergraduate admissions open year-round
- Financial Aid Priority Deadline: March 15
- Graduate program deadlines vary by department
