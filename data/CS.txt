
"Department of Computer Science"

The Computer Science Department at SCSU offers academic programs that prepare students for careers in software development, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and data science. The department emphasizes hands-on learning, real-world projects, and research opportunities. Students also engage in internships, hackathons, and career development activities. The department supports pathways into graduate school and professional certifications.

Key Programs:
- B.S. in Computer Science
- B.S. in Computer Science – Cybersecurity Concentration
- M.S. in Computer Science
- 4+1 Accelerated B.S./M.S. Program
- Minor in Computer Science

Deadlines:
- Rolling admissions
- 4+1 Graduate Entry: Typically junior year application
