Office of Career and Professional Development

helps students and alumni with career planning, resume writing, internships, and job placements. The office offers career counseling, LinkedIn review, mock interviews, career fairs, and employer networking events. Handshake is used as the main job and internship platform.

Key Programs:
- Handshake job search system
- Internships and co-op programs
- Career coaching and resume services
- Employer recruitment events

Deadlines:
- Career Fair: Scheduled each semester
- Resume review: By appointment
